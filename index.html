How it works
  1. You and the enemy each have a 3×4 grid. Cells share labels, so your A1 is opposite the enemy's A1. Row 1 is each side's front row.
  2. Each round you get energy equal to the round number (max 6) and draw a card. Pick a card, then an empty cell on your grid.
  3. The enemy's plays are shown first as dashed ghost cards on its grid, and the forecast shows what will happen if you end the round now.
  4. Every card has a timer. At the end of each round all timers drop by 1. At 0 the effect fires, then the timer resets. Cards fire in the order they were placed.
  5. Attacks hit the opposite card, the opposite row or column, or the strongest card. Targets are picked fresh each tick, after the enemy's plays land and before any effect fires, so they never shift mid-tick. A card at 0 power is destroyed, and a destroyed card never fires.
  6. After effects, each side scores the total power on its own grid. First to 100 wins.
